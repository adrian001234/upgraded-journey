# Episode 1: "Nori and the Giant Crumb" - Shot List (DRAFT 1, 2026-10-10)

Total: 40 shots, about 172 seconds (2m52s).
Shot types: W = wide, M = medium, C = close-up. 16 W, 16 M, 8 C. No two C in a row.
Rule: every shot with Nori must contain the exact words "Nori the snail" plus her full
description from SHOW_BIBLE.md when turned into video prompts.

## ACT 1 - The find (37s)
1. [W 5s] Sunny lawn at sunrise, dew everywhere. Nori slides into frame leaving a shiny trail. A dewdrop falls on one eyestalk and she blinks that eye only. GAG: one-eye blink. SOUND: cheerful music, drip.
2. [M 4s] Nori stretches and yawns; one eyestalk looks left, the other looks right. GAG: cross-eyed stretch. SOUND: boing.
3. [W 4s] Behind her, a huge golden cookie crumb glitters in a sunbeam. She slides straight past without noticing. GAG: she misses the giant thing. SOUND: sparkle.
4. [C 3s] Her eyestalks stop. Slowly, both swivel backwards. GAG: double take. SOUND: record scratch.
5. [M 4s] She turns. The crumb is bigger than she is. Eyestalks stretch out long, mouth makes an O. GAG: eyes pop. SOUND: gasp, sparkle.
6. [M 5s] Attempt 1: she bites the crumb. It is rock hard. Her mouth bounces off and she rubs it. GAG: bounce off. SOUND: boing, ow squeak.
7. [W 4s] Attempt 2: she pushes up to lift it. It does not move and she gets squashed flat like a pancake. GAG: pancake squash. SOUND: splat, squeak.
8. [C 3s] Flat-faced Nori, eyes sliding together, then inflating back to normal. GAG: re-inflate. SOUND: pop.
9. [M 5s] Idea! She balances the crumb on her shell like a hat. It wobbles, then steadies. She strikes a proud pose. GAG: crumb hat. SOUND: wobble, triumphant trumpet.

## ACT 2 - The struggle (58s)
10. [W 5s] Nori sets off with the crumb hat. Far across the lawn we see the goal: a mossy log with a tiny round door. GAG: crumb wobble. SOUND: traveling music.
11. [M 4s] A grassy slope stands in the way. Her eyes look up, up, up, and she gulps. GAG: gulp. SOUND: gulp.
12. [W 5s] Climb try 1: halfway up she slips and slides all the way down her own shiny trail, crumb still on top, happy face. GAG: slide down. SOUND: slide whistle.
13. [M 4s] Climb try 2: nearly at the top, she slips again and spins down. GAG: spin out. SOUND: slide whistle, dizzy.
14. [C 3s] Determined face, eyes narrowing. GAG: the build-up. SOUND: heroic drum roll.
15. [W 6s] Try 3: fake-heroic dash with speed lines and a dust cloud for two seconds, then she runs out of steam and flops over the top of the hill. GAG: sprint then flop. SOUND: hero montage music, thud.
16. [M 4s] She lies flat on her back at the top, panting, the crumb covering her face like a blanket. GAG: crumb blanket. SOUND: tired sigh.
17. [W 4s] From the hilltop she sees the sprinkler in the middle of the lawn, sweeping left and right. It stands between her and the log. GAG: the obstacle. SOUND: tick tick tick.
18. [C 3s] Her eyes sweep left-right-left following the sprinkler like a tennis match. GAG: tennis eyes. SOUND: tick tick.
19. [M 5s] She conducts with her eyestalks counting the rhythm: one... two... Like a video game, she is learning the pattern. GAG: video-game timing. SOUND: counting beeps.
20. [W 5s] Try 1: she dashes too early. The spray blasts her back, shell spinning. The crumb gets soggy and flops over her like a floppy hat. GAG: soggy hat. SOUND: whoosh, splat.
21. [M 4s] Try 2: she freezes halfway. Water drips off her eyestalks. She gives the camera a deadpan look. GAG: deadpan to camera. SOUND: drip, sad trombone.
22. [W 6s] Try 3: perfect timing. She glides through the gap in slow motion as droplets make a rainbow around her, an action-movie dodge. She emerges the other side, soaked and smug. GAG: slow-motion dodge. SOUND: slow-mo whoosh, cheer.

## ACT 3 - The lawn fights back (45s)
23. [W 4s] A shadow passes over the grass. Nori freezes. A sparrow lands in the foreground with a hop. GAG: the villain arrives. SOUND: flutter, thump.
24. [C 3s] The sparrow's big curious eye fills the frame. It tilts its head and looks at the crumb. GAG: curious bird. SOUND: chirp "ooh".
25. [M 4s] Nori and the sparrow face to face. Both look at the crumb, then at each other, then at the crumb. A dry leaf rolls past like a tumbleweed. GAG: western standoff. SOUND: western whistle.
26. [W 5s] The sparrow lunges. Nori runs in dramatic slow motion with epic music, while the sparrow arrives instantly and pecks the crumb. She is still mid-stride. GAG: speed mismatch. SOUND: epic slow-mo music, peck.
27. [M 4s] The crumb is rock hard. The sparrow's beak bounces off it and it hops on one foot, beak vibrating. GAG: sparrow hurts its beak. SOUND: bonk, ow tweet.
28. [C 3s] Nori's eyes narrow. She is annoyed for the first time. Then she pulls in. GAG: tiny anger. SOUND: gulp, zip.
29. [M 4s] She tucks into her shell with the crumb on top. It looks like a lumpy rock. The sparrow tilts its head and pokes it. GAG: pretend rock. SOUND: tap tap.
30. [W 5s] The sparrow pecks harder. The shell tips over and rolls down a gentle slope like a ball, spinning faster, the sparrow chasing. GAG: rolling shell. SOUND: rolling, kazoo.
31. [M 4s] The shell spins like a top. The sparrow follows it with its head, eyes turning into spirals. GAG: dizzy bird. SOUND: dizzy whistle.
32. [W 5s] The sparrow staggers in circles and bonks into a daisy. Petals fall and the daisy lands on its head like a hat. GAG: daisy hat. SOUND: bonk, petals.
33. [M 4s] Nori peeks out one eye, sees the dizzy sparrow, gives a tiny sorry wave and slides on with the crumb back on her shell. GAG: polite escape. SOUND: ding.

## ACT 4 - The payoff (32s)
34. [W 5s] Golden afternoon light. Nori arrives at the mossy log and its tiny round door, tired but beaming, crumb on her shell. GAG: the finish line. SOUND: warm music swells.
35. [M 4s] She tries to go in. The crumb hits the door frame. She looks at the door, the crumb, the door. GAG: it does not fit. SOUND: thunk.
36. [C 3s] One eye squints like a carpenter measuring the crumb against the door. GAG: the sofa-through-the-door moment. SOUND: measuring tape zip.
37. [W 6s] She pushes, turns it sideways, tries upside down. The crumb wedges in the door and she wedges in too, tail wiggling outside. GAG: stuck. SOUND: strain, squeaks.
38. [M 4s] The sparrow arrives with a daisy still on its head. It looks at the stuck crumb. Nori's eyes look nervous. GAG: awkward pause. SOUND: held note.
39. [W 6s] The sparrow gently pecks the crumb. It pops free. Nori shoots through the door like a cork. The sparrow grabs the crumb and flies off happily. GAG: accidental hero. SOUND: pop, whoosh, chirp.
40. [C 4s] Nori peeks from her tiny round window at sunset and waves at the flying sparrow. A single tiny crumb speck is stuck on her shell. She nibbles it happily. GAG: she got a bite after all. SOUND: gentle music, tiny crunch.
